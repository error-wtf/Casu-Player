# Third-party corresponding source offer

MPCASU Player bundles unmodified third-party runtime components, including
Qt, VLC/libVLC, FFmpeg/ffprobe and yt-dlp where listed for a platform.

For components whose LGPL or GPL terms require corresponding source, the
distributor will provide the corresponding source used for the distributed
binary, together with applicable build and configuration information, upon
request where required by the applicable license. This offer remains valid
for at least the period required by that license.

Requests may be submitted to the project owner through the public repository:
https://github.com/error-wtf/Casu-Player

Official upstream locations are provided for convenience:

- Qt: https://www.qt.io/
- VLC/libVLC: https://www.videolan.org/vlc/
- FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/
- yt-dlp: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

These upstream links do not replace the distributor's corresponding-source
obligations where an applicable license requires source under the
distributor's control.

The exact FFmpeg configuration captured from the bundled Windows executable
is included in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/FFmpeg/CONFIGURATION.txt`. It contains
`--enable-gpl`; the detected distribution mode is GPL-2.0-or-later.
